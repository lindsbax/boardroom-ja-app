Step 1 · 16 weeks out

The Readiness Check

Before you approach a single sponsor, get brutally clear on these five things. You don't need every detail listed below — but the more specific you are, the easier the next steps become.

Step 2 · 14 weeks out

Identify Your Target Market

Who wants to meet or sell to the people in your room — and why? Most events support two or three distinct sponsorship categories. Name each one and be specific about the "why."

Category 01e.g. Financial services — reason: your audience are decision-makers with disposable income / business banking needs.
Category 02e.g. Lifestyle / hospitality brand — reason: your audience matches their target demographic and they want brand visibility with this exact room.
Category 03e.g. Professional services — reason: they're selling directly to attendees or want association with the topic/mission.

Replace with your event's actual categories — this is the single biggest driver of who says yes.

Step 3 · 13 weeks out

Identify the Decision Maker

Typically the Marketing Manager, Brand Manager, or Head of Partnerships/CSR — not generic info@ inboxes. Check LinkedIn for title match, and always check your own network first: a warm intro outperforms a cold one every time.

Step 4 · 12 weeks out (3 months)

Send Your Proposal

Reach out directly by email, text, or LinkedIn with your sponsorship proposal attached. Keep it short — the proposal does the selling, your message just gets it opened.
